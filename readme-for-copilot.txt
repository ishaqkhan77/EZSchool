This is a school management and communication platform designed to simplify how schools operate and interact with parents.

The goal is to replace scattered systems like WhatsApp groups, manual attendance, and paper-based records with a centralized, easy-to-use digital solution.

The platform will allow:

Teachers to manage attendance, assignments, and student performance
Parents to track their child’s progress, attendance, and school updates in real-time
Schools to manage staff, students, fees, and academic records efficiently
Direct communication between teachers and parents through a built-in system

It will support multi-branch schools, where each branch is managed under a single account, while keeping all data organized and secure.

The product will be built as a SaaS (Software as a Service) with a monthly subscription model, making it affordable and scalable for schools of different sizes.

The main vision is to:

Improve transparency between schools and parents
Save time by automating daily operations
Provide a reliable and structured system for managing academic data
Digitize school processes in a simple, practical, and accessible way

The focus is on creating a mobile-friendly web application (convertible into an Android app) that is easy for non-technical users, especially in local school environments.
